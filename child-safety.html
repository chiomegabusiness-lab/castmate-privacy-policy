CastMate Child Safety Standards

Last updated: August 9, 2026

CastMate has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation.

Prohibited Content and Conduct

CastMate strictly prohibits content or behavior involving:

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM).
  • Sexual exploitation or sexualization of minors.
  • Grooming, solicitation, or inappropriate contact with minors.
  • Sextortion, trafficking, or threats involving minors.
  • Sharing, requesting, promoting, or linking to exploitative content involving children.

Reporting Child Safety Concerns

Users may report users or user-generated content through CastMate’s in-app reporting features. Reports may also be sent to kuramaartsdeveloper@gmail.com with the subject Child Safety Report.

If a child is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or the appropriate law-enforcement authority.

Enforcement

When CastMate identifies a potential child-safety violation, we may:

  • Remove the offending content.
  • Restrict, suspend, or permanently ban the responsible account.
  • Preserve relevant information where permitted or required by law.
  • Report suspected or confirmed CSAM to the appropriate regional or national authorities where legally required.

Cooperation With Authorities

CastMate complies with applicable child-safety laws and cooperates with authorized law-enforcement agencies and child-protection organizations when legally required.
